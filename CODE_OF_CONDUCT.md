# Code of Conduct

Switchyard follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work rather than the person.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable in issues, pull
requests, discussions or any other project space.

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks these expectations, contact the maintainer
privately through the email address on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/joaoh82), or
use GitHub's _Report content_ feature. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation where needed.
